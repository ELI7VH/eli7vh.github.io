The Developer Promise

Trust in the tools you use to express yourself is paramount. For over a decade, spanning audio production, voice acting, generative art, and web development, I've watched brilliant ideas die because the software in the way was too cumbersome, too fleeting, or abandoned by its creators.

I build to solve that. I build because I am a creative first. I know exactly what it feels like to lose the spark while wrestling with a broken interface. Every line of code I write is dedicated to removing the friction between your intent and your output.

The Ecosystem

I maintain a growing framework of open-source utilities and creative interfaces designed for longevity. These tools aren't side projects; they are my calling.

  • Dank Inc. Libraries

    Open-source, lightweight Typescript creative coding packages (Banger, Sketchy, Lewps, Super-Mouse).

  • Generative Art Systems

    Frameworks marrying raw canvas, p5.js, and Three.js for visual exploration without bloat.

  • Audio & Music Tooling

    From Reaper JSFX plugins to browser-based synthesized noise engines designed for performers.

  • The Creative Journal

    Dedicated tracking and stage-building software designed explicitly to support an artist's lifecycle.
